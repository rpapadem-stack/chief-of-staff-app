Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-19.

Chief of staff is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer, for that person's own use. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, given on Google's sign-in screen, Chief of staff accesses the owner's own Gmail messages and Google Calendar events through Google's APIs.

How that data is used

  • Only to do what the owner asks: summarize mail, save drafts, send messages the owner approved, and read or update their calendar.
  • It is processed on the owner's own computer. Sign-in tokens are kept in that computer's system keychain.
  • When the owner asks a question about their mail or calendar, the relevant text may be sent to the AI model the owner uses to answer it, under that provider's terms. It is not used for anything else.

What it never does

  • It does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising.
  • It does not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.
  • No person other than the owner reads the owner's data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Chief of staff's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Chief of staff's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections, and delete its local files from their computer.

Contact

Questions go to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
