Chief of staff

A personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer.

Chief of staff is a private tool, built for one person's own use. It helps its owner plan their day, keep track of priorities, and work with their own email and calendar.

What it does with Google

When its owner signs in with their own Google account, Chief of staff can:

  • read their Gmail, so it can summarize what needs attention;
  • save draft replies, and send email only when its owner asks for that message;
  • read their Google Calendar and add or change events on it.

It runs on its owner's own computer. There is no server, no shared database and no public sign-up. It is not offered to the public.
